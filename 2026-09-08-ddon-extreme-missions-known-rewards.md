# Dragon's Dogma Online Extreme Missions: Known Missions and Rewards

**Research date:** 2026-09-08  
**Scope:** Dragon's Dogma Online (DDON) Extreme Missions (EM)

## Important qualification

This is a conservative record of what has been established from surviving Japanese-language archives and search results. It is **not** a guaranteed complete historical mission or reward database. DDON changed its mission classifications and reward tables across seasons, and some former eight-player Grand Missions were later integrated into the EM system.

Reward behavior also varied by mission. First-clear rewards were generally increased once per day, with the daily reset reported as 5:00 AM. Some missions had unique rare materials, while others also awarded ordinary materials, experience, Rift points, or Blood Orbs.

### Reward details established for selected missions

| Mission | Documented reward information |
|---|---|
| 戦徒と共に (*Together with the War Disciples*) | 習得の書【友癒】 (*Learning Manual: [Friend Healing]*) is reported as a reward. This mission uses a solo/paired-with-Pawns format rather than normal party play. |
| 大竜晶破壊戦：主無き古城 (*Great Dragon Crystal Destruction Battle: The Lordless Ancient Castle*) | 滅びへ向かう鍵石 (*Key Stone Toward Ruin*) is reported as a clear reward. |

| 狂宴に舞う蒼影 (*Azure Shadow Dancing in the Mad Banquet*) | 堕落の禁角 (*Fallen Forbidden Horn*) and 異界の雫 (*Drop of the Otherworld*) are reported as principal rewards. |

These entries should be treated as documented examples until verified against an original mission reward table.

## Classification notes

- EM began as a four-player version of the older Grand Mission-style content.
- By Season 3.0, Grand Mission content had been integrated into the EM system, leaving both four-player and eight-player EM classifications in some references.
- Temporary/event EMs, special EMs, and missions associated with レスタニア追懐戦記 (*Lestania Reminiscence Chronicle*) existed in addition to the permanent missions.
- The “高難度” (*High Difficulty*) entries and multiple 大竜晶破壊戦 (*Great Dragon Crystal Destruction Battle*) scenarios should not automatically be counted as separate base missions.

## What is not yet known with certainty

The following has not been reconstructed reliably:

1. A complete chronological list of every permanent and limited-time EM.
2. Every ordinary reward-table item for each mission.
3. Exact quantities, drop rates, and first-clear versus repeat-clear differences.
4. A complete reward table for the later endgame missions and all eight-player missions.
5. Whether every archive uses the same English naming or groups the same content under the same EM category.
